SCENARIO 1

Dynamic Speed Recovery & Safe Distance Control

Recovering lost time safely by boosting trailing train speed while strictly maintaining mandatory distance buffers.

⚡ Speed Optimization & ETA Recovery

  • Controlled Acceleration: When the trailing coaching train is running behind schedule, AUTONEX calculates clear track segments and permits speed recovery up to max safe section limits.
  • Live Location Synchronicity: Both train coordinates are continuously refreshed across central signal server and loco-cab display.
  • ETA Forecast Compression: Reduces accumulated delay by 15-25% over open track corridors without risking signal overruns.

🛡️ Fail-Safe Collision Protection

  • Mandatory Safety Distance Boundary: Real-time braking curves recalculate continuously based on current train speed, weight, and weather.
  • Automated Emergency Throttling: If the gap drops below the safety threshold, the system throttles back speed automatically.
  • 0% Collision Guarantee: Safety distance telemetry operates with fail-safe interlocks independent of human error.

SCENARIO 2

Pre-emptive Loop Line Routing & Dynamic Station Halt

Intelligent traffic diversion 20-30 minutes in advance and dynamic station halt relocation to prevent bottlenecks.

SCENARIO 2A

🔄 Pre-emptive 20-30 Min Loop Line Shunting

  • Early Distance Detection: When a Coaching train approaches a slower Freight train from behind, AUTONEX detects distance gap narrowing 20–30 minutes in advance.
  • Smooth Loop Line Insertion: The slower train is pre-routed into a loop line well before the express arrives, avoiding sudden emergency stopping.
  • Uninterrupted Overtake: High-speed coaching trains maintain momentum without encountering yellow/red signal delays.
SCENARIO 2B

🚉 Dynamic Station Halt Relocation

  • Bottleneck Avoidance: If halting a train at an upcoming congested station will choke the track section, AUTONEX dynamically shifts its operational halt to the next station.
  • Real-time Delay Screen Display: Live delay status (+ minutes) and updated ETA schedules are instantly rendered on station & controller dashboards.
  • Zero Cascading Delays: Prevents train congestion queues at small stations.
